Privacy Policy

Real Estate Ranger

Effective Date: June 02, 2026

We at Real Estate Ranger respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and phone number when you create an account.
  • Property & Tenant Data: Property addresses, tenant names, lease details, rent amounts, and related records.
  • Financial Information: Payment records, expenses, and cash flow data you enter.
  • Photos & Documents: Move-in/move-out inspection photos, receipts, and lease documents you upload.
  • Usage Data: App interactions, features used, and crash reports.
  • Notifications: Device token if you enable push notifications.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core features (lease generation, rent tracking, inspections, expense management).
  • To improve the app and fix bugs.
  • To send important notifications (rent due reminders, maintenance tasks, etc.).
  • To generate reports and cash flow summaries.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share data only in these cases:

  • With service providers (e.g., cloud hosting) who help operate the app.
  • When required by law.
  • In the event of a business transfer (merger, acquisition, etc.).

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. However, no system is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Access, update, or delete your data by contacting us.
  • Request export of your data.
  • Opt out of notifications at any time in app settings.

Children’s Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13.
