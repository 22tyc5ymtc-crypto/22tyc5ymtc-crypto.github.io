Privacy Policy

Real Estate Ranger

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Last updated: November 18, 2026

We at Real Estate Ranger respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address. Sign-in is through Sign in with Apple, as described below.
  • Property & Tenant Data: Property addresses, tenant names, lease details, rent amounts, and related records.
  • Financial Information: Payment records, expenses, and cash flow data you enter.
  • Photos & Documents: Move-in/move-out inspection photos, receipts, and lease documents you upload.
  • Usage Data: App interactions, features used, and crash reports.
  • Notifications: Device token if you enable push notifications.
  • Advertising Data: Device identifiers (for example, IDFA where permitted), IP address, coarse location, and ad interaction data, as described in Advertising below.
  • Purchase Information: Subscription status and transaction information needed to manage Pro access, as described in Purchases and Subscriptions below.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core features (lease generation, rent tracking, inspections, expense management).
  • To improve the app and fix bugs.
  • To send important notifications (rent due reminders, maintenance tasks, etc.).
  • To generate reports and cash flow summaries.
  • To show ads in the free version of the app.
  • To process subscriptions and manage Pro access.
  • To provide optional AI features you choose to use.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads through Google AdMob, including interstitial and rewarded ads. Google may collect device identifiers (for example, IDFA where permitted), IP address, coarse location, and ad interaction data.

We ask for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt and, where required, Google’s consent message (UMP). If you decline tracking, ads are still shown but are non-personalized.

For more information, see Google’s advertising policy.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Optional Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your card details. RevenueCat processes purchase status and transaction information, along with an anonymous app user ID, so we can manage your subscription access.

See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Account and Cloud Storage

You sign in with Sign in with Apple. We receive your name and email address, or Apple’s private relay email if you choose to hide your email. We do not receive or store a password.

App data is stored in Google Firebase (authentication and cloud database) and is tied to your account. See Firebase’s privacy information.

AI Features

If you use optional AI features, the text you submit is sent to our AI service provider to generate a response and is not used by us for advertising.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share data only in these cases:

  • With service providers who help operate the app, including Google (Firebase and AdMob), RevenueCat, Apple, and our AI service provider, as described in this policy.
  • When required by law.
  • In the event of a business transfer (merger, acquisition, etc.).

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption, including in Google Firebase as described above. However, no system is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Permanently delete your account and associated cloud data anytime in the app under Settings > Delete Account, or by emailing RealEstateRangerLLC@gmail.com.
  • Access or update your data by contacting us.
  • Request export of your data.
  • Opt out of notifications at any time in app settings.

Children’s Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13.
